- **Signing in with your email works again:** Spotify's sign-in pages could tell they were opened inside an app and turned the email-code login away. SoundScream now signs in as the desktop browser it presents itself as.
- **Autoplay on the player:** the new infinity button next to Share turns autoplay (similar songs after your music ends) on or off without going to Settings. It's green when on.
- **A cleaner queue:** shuffle, repeat, autoplay and the sleep timer are plain icons at the bottom of the queue, with the list fading in behind them. The end of the queue says whether similar songs follow or playback stops.
- **Autoplay off now stops where it should:** a song you played on its own stops at its end and goes back to its start, instead of Spotify moving on to another song.

**Install:** download `SoundScream.apk` and install it over your current copy. It's built for arm64 phones.
