- **Taps that don't work after a network drop:** if Spotify's hidden player stops responding after your connection drops, tapping a song, Next or Previous now reloads it within about 6 seconds and plays what you asked for. Before, the tap was lost and the screen jumped back until you closed the app.
- **Fewer "Spotify is limiting playback" pauses:** when you skip a lot, skips are spaced out (2 s, then 4 s after 3 skips in 30 s, then 6 s after 6), and taps in between add up into one jump. The screen still shows each tap right away.

**Install:** download `SoundScream.apk` and install it over your current copy. It's built for arm64 phones.
